## 6. Product lines in detail

### 6.1 CleanLine
- **Purpose:** commercial-grade aluminum landscape edging, "a complete series of commercial grade aluminum landscape edgings". Built to hold up to professional lawn maintenance. Uses include bed edging, gravel and aggregate walkways, and similar applications.
- **Sizes (thickness x height):** 1/8" x 3", 1/8" x 4", 5/32" x 4", 3/16" x 4", 1/8" x 5.5", 3/16" x 5.5". The 1/8" x 3" size comes in 8 ft only, in Mill and Black DuraFlex only.
- **Heights:** 3", 4", 5.5".
- **Piece lengths:** 8 ft and 16 ft.
- **Finishes:** Mill, Black DuraFlex, Green DuraFlex, Bronze DuraFlex, Black Anodized. (The spec sheet calls the black finish "paint", which is a minor naming conflict.)
- **Connection:** patented stakeless **Snap-Down** connection that "snaps down to permanently interlock adjacent edging sections" and needs no extra parts. A retailer advises deducting 8 in. per section for the overlap when estimating quantity (retailer data).
- **Stakes:** 12 in. interlocking aluminum stakes; 3 per 8 ft section and 5 per 16 ft section. Upgrade lengths of 18 in. and 24 in. are available. An older Sweets short spec lists the standard stake as 12 in., 0.10 in. thick, with optional 0.125 in. thick stakes in 16 in., 18 in. and 24 in.
- **Install:** dig the trench 1 in. deeper than the bottom of the edging. Set the top 1/2 in. above compacted finish grade on the turf side. Drive the stakes through the loops until locked.
- **URL:** https://permaloc.com/edging/cleanline/ (spec: https://permaloc.com/downloads/specs/cl10part.pdf)

### 6.2 CleanLine XL
- **Purpose:** heavy-duty version, preferred for heavy foot traffic, very large aggregates or demanding conditions. The 12 in. height is often used for curves or raised beds.
- **Sizes:** 3/16" thick in 5", 6" and 8" heights, plus a 12" height (thickness of the 12" NOT FOUND).
- **Piece lengths:** 8 ft and 16 ft.
- **Finishes:** Mill, Black DuraFlex, and Bronze DuraFlex (8 ft only). The older catalog also lists Green DuraFlex, which conflicts with the spec sheet.
- **Connection:** **XLR Adaptor** joins sections to avoid weak points at the joint. For a permanent joint, screw the adaptor to the edging.
- **Stakes:** heavy-duty 18 in. interlocking aluminum stakes. Each 8 ft section includes 3 stakes and 3 XLR adaptors. 24 in. stakes are available. Stake count per 16 ft section: NOT FOUND.
- **Install:** dig the trench 1 in. deeper than the bottom of the edging. Set the top at most 1/2 in. above the surface material; edging set higher than 1/2 in. above grade may be a trip hazard in foot-traffic areas.
- **Accessories:** 5" XL corner (DuraFlex) is listed.
- **URL:** https://permaloc.com/edging/cleanline-xl/ (spec: https://permaloc.com/downloads/specs/xl10part.pdf)

### 6.3 ProLine

## 7. Installation guidance (general landscape edging)

1. Call to locate underground utilities before digging.
2. Lay out the line with string or a garden hose, and clear roots and rocks from the trench path.
3. Dig the trench about **1 in. deeper** than the bottom of the edging.
4. Set the edging so the top is about **1/2 in. above finished grade** (turf side). Higher than 1/2 in. may be a trip hazard in foot-traffic areas.
5. Join sections with the stakeless connection: snap down (CleanLine, ProLine), slide or telescope (ProSlide; ProSlide LT needs at least 2 in. of overlap), or use the XLR adaptor (CleanLine XL). Make sure sections overlap so no gaps open for weeds or separation.
6. Drive the stakes through the stake loops or pockets until locked. No stake is needed at the joint.
7. Backfill both sides and compact.
- **90-degree corners:** cut from the **bottom** about halfway through the edging, then bend the free end around a board or brick. Cutting from the bottom keeps the top bead solid and safe.
- **Cutting tool:** a hacksaw is recommended.
- **Slopes:** use the Grade Change Connector.
- **Other resources:** product-specific instructions and videos are at https://permaloc.com/installation-instructions/. A permeable pavement guide is at https://permaloc.com/downloads/installation/Permeable%20Installation.pdf. CAD details are at https://permaloc.com/cad-by-application/, tech specs at https://permaloc.com/technical-specifcations/, and design tools at https://permaloc.com/design-tools/.

## 8. FAQ (from https://permaloc.com/faq/)

- **What lengths?** Landscape edging comes in 8 ft or 16 ft pieces. L-shaped hardscape and green-build restraints come in 8 ft only.
- **Are stakes included?** Yes. Each 8 ft piece includes 3 stakes and each 16 ft piece includes 5 (PermaStrip 16 ft includes 8 per its spec; CleanLine XL uses 18 in. stakes).
- **Stake length?** 12 in. standard. 18 in. and 24 in. stakes are made for special applications.
- **Can I add stakes?** An 8 ft piece has no extra stake pockets. For a 16 ft piece, 3 additional stakes can be bought.
- **What circle can one piece make?** An 8 ft piece makes a circle of 7'4" circumference, 2'4" diameter, 1'2" radius. A 16 ft piece makes a circle about 4'11" in diameter.
- **Will it rust?** The aluminum "will not rust, rot, or break down in any way."
- **Will it heave out of the ground?** The extrusion is designed to resist frost heave, the connections are engineered not to separate underground, and the 12 in. barbed stakes hold it in place.
- **Can I mow over it?** When installed as recommended, the top sits below mower-blade height, so the mower can run over the edging.
- **How do I make a 90-degree corner?** Cut partway (about halfway) through from the bottom, then bend it around a board or brick.
- **How do I cut it?** With a hacksaw.
- **Hilly yard?** Use the patented Grade Change Connector.
- **How much do I need?** Measure with a measuring wheel, or lay a rope or hose of known length along the bed and count lengths.
- **Can I buy online?** Not from Permaloc directly; many distributors sell online.
- **Price?** Get pricing from your local dealer.
- **Questions?** Call product specialists at 1-800-356-9660.
